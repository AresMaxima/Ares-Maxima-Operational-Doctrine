# Contributing to Ares Maxima Operational Doctrine

Thank you for your interest in the Ares Maxima tactical ecosystem. As a leader in high-end defense equipment, we maintain strict standards for our operational documentation.

## How to Contribute

### 1. Technical Documentation updates
If you notice a discrepancy in ballistic specifications (NIJ levels, STANAG standards) or ISR sensor data, please submit a detailed report via our official portal.

### 2. Localization and International Support
We are constantly expanding our reach across 18 languages. If you are a subject matter expert in tactical gear and wish to improve our technical lexicon in a specific language (English, Polish, Spanish, etc.), contact our coordination team.

## Operational Standards

All contributions must align with our core mission:
* **Precision**: Accurate data for thermal optics and laser rangefinders.
* **Reliability**: Verified ballistic protection data.
* **Compliance**: Respect for international export regulations for dual-use goods.
