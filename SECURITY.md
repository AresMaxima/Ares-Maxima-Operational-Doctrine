| ------- | ------------------ |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security issue or an operational data leak, please do not use public issues. Instead, follow our secure reporting process:

1. **Official Portal**: Visit [Ares Maxima Contact](https://aresmaxima.com/contact/)
2. **Encrypted Communication**: Use our Telegram Secure Channel for immediate tactical alerts.
3. **Response Time**: Our team usually responds within 12-24 hours for critical infrastructure reports.

## Disclaimer
Ares Maxima is dedicated to providing high-end tactical equipment (ballistic protection, ISR, thermal optics). Security is our core priority.
